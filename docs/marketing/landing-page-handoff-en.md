## 4. How It Works Section

**Section heading:** "How it works"

### 3 steps (layout: cards or timeline)

| Step | Icon | Headline | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔗 | **Connect in 2 minutes** | No new app to download. Open the bot on Telegram, connect your bank via SePay or email forwarding. The bot guides you step by step. |
| 2 | 🤖 | **Bot categorizes automatically** | Transactions from known merchants are auto-categorized. Unknown ones? The bot asks you with one-tap buttons — done in a second. |
| 3 | 📊 | **Reports when you need them** | Type /today for today's spending. Type /status for your monthly overview. The bot sends a daily summary every evening — automatically. |

### Flow diagram (for illustration)

```
Bank transaction
       ↓
  SePay webhook / Email forward
       ↓
  Bot receives & auto-categorizes
       ↓
  ┌─ Rule match → Auto-categorize + Notify
  └─ No match → Ask user with tap buttons
       ↓
  Reports updated in real-time
```

## 9. FAQ Section

**Section heading:** "Frequently asked questions"
**Layout:** Accordion (click to expand)

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **How long is the free plan?** | Forever. The Free plan includes 45 transactions/month and 1 bank account. Enough for basic personal tracking. |
| 2 | **Do I need to install an app?** | No. You just need Telegram or Discord — open the bot and start. |
| 3 | **Which banks are supported?** | Any bank connected via SePay (most Vietnamese banks). Additionally, direct email parsing for: Techcombank, Cake, ACB, Sacombank, BIDV, MB. More banks added regularly. |
| 4 | **Is my financial data safe?** | The bot does NOT access your bank account. It only receives transaction notifications via webhook or email. Data is encrypted, account numbers are never stored. |
| 5 | **How does the 14-day trial work?** | Every new user automatically gets Pro for 14 days, no card required. After 14 days, you're downgraded to Free. All your data is preserved. |
| 6 | **How do I cancel?** | Type /cancel in the bot. Full refund within the first 7 days, no questions asked. |
| 7 | **What is SePay?** | SePay is a popular bank webhook service — when a bank transaction occurs, SePay sends a real-time notification to the bot. If you don't have SePay, the bot guides you through setup, or you can use email forwarding instead. |
| 8 | **What if I don't want to use SePay?** | Use email forwarding: forward your bank notification emails to the bot. No SePay needed. |
| 9 | **How does Personal/Business split work?** | The Business plan auto-separates by bank account (Bank A = business, Bank B = personal). You can also manually tag any transaction. |
| 10 | **Can I export for my accountant?** | Yes. Business syncs real-time with Google Sheets. Pro exports CSV. |
