## 🔗 SIMILARITY TO MMW: 3/5
**Overlap:** One-tap manual entry, cloud persistence, multi-device sync. **Gap:** No bot/messaging, no bank sync, no collaboration. MMW's Telegram-first approach is inversely opposite to 1Money's app-centric design.

## 💡 LESSONS FOR MMW
1. **Speed wins** — Users love frictionless capture (1-tap model validates speed-first design)
2. **Crypto appeal** — Early crypto support in expense tracking still rare and differentiating
3. **Avoid feature bloat** — 1Money's minimalism is its strength; don't chase YNAB's complexity
4. **Sync strategy** — Cloud sync critical but must not require another account/login overhead
