# 📱 Fast Budget — Android-native Italian budget tracker with web app

**💰 Free + Premium (exact price [unverified])** | **👥 ~100k+ Android users (Play Store)** | **⭐ 4.3/5** | **🌍 Global (Italian origin)**

## 🎯 SIGNATURE FEATURES (top 3)

### 1. Debt/Credit Tracking as First-Class
- **How:** Dedicated page for loans to banks/friends/companies; separate UI from transactions
- **Why:** Households often mix expense tracking with borrowed-money tracking; single pane visibility
- **Evidence:** [FastBudget.app features](https://fastbudget.app/) | [Play Store listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.blodhgard.easybudget)

### 2. Calendar View with Forward-Look
- **How:** Day-by-day calendar showing profits/costs; view scheduled transactions for next N days
- **Why:** Shows cash flow timing, not just totals; helps avoid overdrafts
- **Evidence:** [FastBudget Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.blodhgard.easybudget) | [FastBudget.app calendar feature](https://fastbudget.app/)

### 3. Subscription Manager + Auto-Backup
- **How:** Track recurring subscriptions; view monthly + annual spend; auto-backup to Dropbox; passcode protection
- **Why:** Subscription fatigue is real; auto-backup prevents data loss (important for Android which lacks iCloud)
- **Evidence:** [FastBudget Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.blodhgard.easybudget)

## 🎁 UNIQUE INTERACTIONS (max 3)
1. **Debt tracking as workflow** — Separate loans/credits page (not just "Loan" category); users report this helpful
2. **Calendar-first cash flow** — Shows day-by-day profit/loss + future scheduled txns; unique angle on cash-flow visibility

## 🔗 SIMILARITY TO MMW: 2/5
**Overlap:** Multi-account, simple UI, Android-native. **Gap:** No messaging, no collaboration, no bank sync at scale. MMW's Telegram + web design is opposite to Fast Budget's native-app-only architecture.

## 💡 LESSONS FOR MMW
1. **Debt tracking is neglected** — Fast Budget's dedicated loans page is UX innovation; most trackers bury it in categories
2. **Calendar view validates time-dimension** — Users care about "when is money due" not just "how much was spent"
3. **Subscription tracking is a TAM** — Even niche feature (reminder before renewal) drives retention
4. **Android matters** — Fast Budget's Italian origin shows strong Android play; iOS still dominates globally but Android expansion underestimated
5. **Web app must match native** — Fast Budget's web app is minimal; customers expect feature parity (or they churn)
