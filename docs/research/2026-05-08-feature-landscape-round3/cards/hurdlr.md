# 📱 Hurdlr — Gig/Freelancer/Solopreneur Mileage + Tax Tracker

**💰 Free / $9.99/mo Premium / $200/yr Pro** | **⭐ 4.3-4.7** | **🌍 US (iOS/Android)**

---

## 🎯 SIGNATURE FEATURES (Top 3)

### 1. GPS-Powered Automatic Mileage Tracking
- **How**: Real-time GPS logs trips automatically; users swipe-categorize as Business/Personal; learns rules over time
- **Why**: Gig workers (Uber/Lyft/DoorDash drivers) waste time manually logging miles; Hurdlr eliminates friction with background automation + accuracy
- **Evidence**: [Hurdlr University — Integrations](https://university.hurdlr.com/en/articles/8496412-how-do-i-link-integrations-like-uber-stripe-square-etc-to-hurdlr), [Fit Small Business Review](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/hurdlr-review/), [Best Mileage Tracker 2025](https://millancpa.com/insights/mileage-tracking-automation-apps-and-leveraging-schedule-c)

### 2. Real-Time Quarterly + Annual Tax Estimates (Schedule C Ready)
- **How**: Aggregates income/expenses/mileage, applies IRS rates (58¢/mile 2026), outputs quarterly federal+state tax due, Schedule C export to tax preparer
- **Why**: Solopreneurs live in tax uncertainty; Hurdlr makes deductions concrete + timely, not a Q4 surprise
- **Evidence**: [The College Investor Review](https://thecollegeinvestor.com/24005/hurdlr-review/), [MoneyWise](https://moneywise.com/employment/hurdlr), [Fit Small Business](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/hurdlr-review/)

### 3. Multi-Platform Revenue Auto-Sync (Stripe, Square, PayPal, Uber)
- **How**: Direct integrations with 9,500+ banks + Stripe, Square, PayPal, Shopify, Airbnb; auto-tag income as Business
- **Why**: Freelancers juggle 3+ income streams; manual entry is error-prone; auto-sync eliminates duplicate-entry friction
- **Evidence**: [Hurdlr University — Integrations](https://university.hurdlr.com/en/articles/8496412-how-do-i-link-integrations-like-uber-stripe-square-etc-to-hurdlr), [College Investor Review](https://thecollegeinvestor.com/24005/hurdlr-review/), [Mileagewise Review](https://www.mileagewise.com/mileiq-alternative/hurdlr-review/)

## 🔴 GAPS (Top 3)

1. **No Multi-Business Support**: Sole proprietorship focus. Can't separate multiple business entities (LLC + Consulting + Uber). Ceiling: $1M ARR single-biz operators.
   - Evidence: Feature reviews across Capterra, G2, Fit Small Business

## 🔗 SIMILARITY TO MMW: **5/5 stars** ⭐

**Hurdlr is the closest competitor to MyMoneyWent's solopreneur ICP.** Both target independent contractors, both emphasize personal+business split (P/B tagging), both offer free-to-premium models, both avoid full accounting-software overhead. Hurdlr's GPS mileage is unique; MMW can differentiate via Telegram/mobile-native, no app install friction, and horizontal (all income types) vs vertical (gig-first).

---

## 💡 LESSONS FOR MMW

1. **Mileage GPS is a Solopreneur Win**: Hurdlr's killer feature is automated context (GPS). MMW on Telegram can't do GPS, but could offer **quick mileage entry** via voice ("logged 12 business miles to client meeting today"). Reduce friction; don't compete on automation.

2. **Tax Readiness = Differentiation**: Hurdlr's Schedule C export is not flashy, but solopreneurs remember it at tax time. MMW should surface **quarterly tax summary** prominently ("Estimated Q2 tax: $1,240; set aside to Taxes Pocket"). Tie engagement to tax calendar.

3. **Multi-Platform Income Sync is Table Stakes**: Hurdlr's Stripe+Square+PayPal integrations are expected, not surprising. MMW via Telegram faces **integration complexity** — prioritize top 5 (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Airbnb, one more) and do those well, not all weakly.

4. **Avoid Collaboration Overreach**: Hurdlr skips household features (single-user). MyMoneyWent could offer single-user P/B split initially, add **team invoice review** (accountant access) in Phase 2. Don't dilute solopreneur focus.
