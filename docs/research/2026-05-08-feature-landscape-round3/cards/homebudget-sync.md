# 📱 HomeBudget with Sync — Family-first multi-device expense manager

**💰 Free (Lite: 20 txns) | Paid Pro (platform-per-purchase)** | **👥 Established household users** | **⭐ 4.5/5** | **🌍 Global, multi-platform**

## 🎯 SIGNATURE FEATURES (top 3)

### 1. Family Sync (OTA Multi-Device Sync)
- **How:** 2+ household devices sync changes over WiFi/3G/4G automatically; all users see same budget in real-time
- **Why:** Household financial collaboration without manual re-entry; enables couples/families to co-manage
- **Evidence:** [Anishu official docs](https://www.anishu.com/homebudget.html) | [App Store review](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/homebudget-with-sync/id306257910) | [YourMoney review](https://www.yourmoney.com/saving-banking/app-review-homebudget-with-sync/)

### 2. Cross-Platform Consistency (5 OSes)
- **How:** Single subscription syncs iPhone/iPad/Android/Mac/Windows; data parity across all
- **Why:** No vendor lock-in; works in mixed-OS households (common globally)
- **Evidence:** [Anishu feature page](https://www.anishu.com/homebudget.html) | [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/homebudget-with-sync/id578747615?mt=12)

### 3. Bills & Account Management
- **How:** Dedicated Bills-due tracker; reconciliation across accounts; account balance monitoring
- **Why:** Prevents late payments; separates recurring from one-time expenses
- **Evidence:** [Anishu product guide](https://www.anishu.com/homebudget.html) | [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/homebudget/reviews)

## 🔗 SIMILARITY TO MMW: 2/5
**Overlap:** Multi-user, household focus, simple UI. **Gap:** No messaging integration, no cloud-native design, lacks bank sync. MMW's Telegram-first architecture is fundamentally different from HomeBudget's native-app-centric approach.

## 💡 LESSONS FOR MMW
1. **Family mode validates household TAM** — HomeBudget's most praised feature is family sync; household finances > solo users
2. **Sync UX matters** — Manual Family Sync OTA refresh is less ideal than push notifications; MMW's webhook-based design superior
3. **Bills as separate workflow** — HomeBudget's dedicated Bills-due section (not just categorized transactions) users appreciate
4. **Cross-platform friction** — Requiring separate purchases per OS (iOS ≠ Android ≠ Mac) is adoption blocker; MMW's web-first avoids this
