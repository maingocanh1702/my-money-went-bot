### 2. Auto-Categorization + Merchant Intelligence

- **Why:** Removes friction; merchant database learns user patterns

### 3. Bank Sync + 70-Country Reach
- **How:** Connects to 15,000+ banks globally; auto-syncs transactions; supports 70 countries
- **Why:** Global reach for expat/traveler users; aggregation without manual entry

## 🎁 UNIQUE INTERACTIONS (max 3)
1. **WallyGPT conversational UI** — First expense tracker to use LLM for budgeting advice (not just insights); users report "brutally honest" feedback is motivating
2. **Auto-categorization + merchant DB** — Works without configuration; appeals to non-technical users

## 🔴 GAPS (max 3)
1. **No collaboration** — Single-user only; cannot split expenses or invite family
2. **AI hallucination risk** — Users express skepticism about GPT advice for critical financial decisions (noted in reviews)
3. **Multi-currency behind paywall** — $7.99 in-app purchase required; basic feature in Buxfer/others free tier

## 🔗 SIMILARITY TO MMW: 3.5/5
**Overlap:** AI-assisted, conversational UX, bank sync, multi-language. **Gap:** No messaging platform (Wally is app-only), no collaboration, no business features. MMW's Telegram-first + async design is orthogonal to Wally's app-centric model.

## 💡 LESSONS FOR MMW
1. **Conversational AI validates UX** — WallyGPT's chatbot reduces learning curve vs traditional dashboards; MMW could layer this (e.g., "Ask bot for budget recommendations")
2. **Auto-categorization is table-stakes** — Users expect this now; manual categorization is friction
3. **Privacy > OpenAI credibility** — Wally's "data never trained" policy resonates vs ChatGPT's public concern; MMW must promise transparent data handling
4. **Bank sync still differentiating** — Wally's 15k banks is strong, but 20k (Buxfer) shows maturity; MMW's value-add is sync + messaging workflow
5. **Multi-currency as premium feature** — Wally paywalls it ($7.99); indicates willingness to pay for multi-currency (future MMW feature)
6. **Avatar/personality matters** — "Love the Wally mascot" cited in reviews; emotional connection drives retention
