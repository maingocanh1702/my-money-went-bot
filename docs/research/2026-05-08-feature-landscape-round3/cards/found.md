# 📱 Found — All-in-One Banking + Bookkeeping for Self-Employed

## 🎯 SIGNATURE FEATURES (Top 3)

### 1. Unified Business Bank Account + Auto-Categorized Bookkeeping
- **How**: User opens business checking account with Found (Lead Bank, FDIC $250K). Every swipe categorizes expense automatically via ML. No separate accounting software needed.
- **Why**: Self-employed tired of switching between bank app + Stripe + QuickBooks. Found consolidates 3 apps into 1. Instant ledger on every transaction.
- **Evidence**: [Found Official — Banking + Bookkeeping](https://found.com/), [Found Bookkeeping Guide](https://found.com/help/bookkeeping/guide-to-bookkeeping), [NerdWallet Review](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/banking/reviews/found-business-checking)

### 2. Automatic Tax Withholding + Quarterly Tax Set-Aside
- **How**: Found calculates estimated quarterly taxes in real-time as income arrives. Auto-transfers % of each deposit into "Taxes Pocket" (virtual sub-account). User can pay federal taxes directly in-app for Schedule C filers.
- **Why**: Solopreneurs underestimate quarterly tax burden; Found eliminates surprise $5K Q4 bills by **visibly setting aside money weekly**. Liquidity management at banking layer.
- **Evidence**: [Found — Tax Planning](https://found.com/taxes), [Found Fee Schedule](https://found.com/legal/fee-schedule-lead), [Self-Employed.com Review](https://www.selfemployed.com/best-expense-tracking-apps-for-self-employed-2024/)

### 3. Professional Invoice Generation + Tracking
- **How**: Create unlimited invoices directly in-app (free, no 3rd-party integrations). Track payment status. Link invoices to expenses for P&L clarity.
- **Why**: Freelancers switching between Wave/Stripe/Square + banking is mental overhead. Found invoices live in same ecosystem as expenses + bank balances.
- **Evidence**: [Found — Bookkeeping](https://found.com/bookkeeping), [Business.org Review](https://www.business.org/finance/banking/found-business-banking-review/), [Found Pricing & Features](https://found.com/)

## 🎁 UNIQUE INTERACTIONS (Top 3)

1. **Pockets (Virtual Sub-Accounts)**: Create separate "buckets" within Found account (Taxes, Operating Expenses, Emergency Fund). Auto-allocate % of every deposit to Taxes Pocket. Reduces complexity of segregating funds.
   - Evidence: [Found Features](https://found.com/), [Pricing page](https://found.com/)

2. **Automatic Tax Withholding on Deposits**: % of every income deposit is automatically moved to Taxes Pocket. Solves the "Where do I put tax money?" problem. Paid quarterly via built-in tax payment.
   - Evidence: [Found — Tax Planning](https://found.com/taxes)

3. **Schedule C Report Generation**: Auto-fills Schedule C form fields from Found transaction history. Exports as PDF or sends to tax preparer. Reduces tax prep friction to 30 minutes instead of 4 hours.
   - Evidence: [Found — Tax Planning](https://found.com/taxes), [NerdWallet Review](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/banking/reviews/found-business-checking)

## 🔴 GAPS (Top 3)

1. **Business-Only Positioning = Limited TAM**: Found explicitly rejects personal accounts. No household management (couples with 1 business + personal expenses). Ceiling: solopreneurs filing Schedule C only (~27M US, but competing set much smaller).
   - Evidence: Search results show "self-employed only" design philosophy across all official pages

2. **Limited Integrations**: No Stripe app Marketplace. No Zapier/API access (implies no developer story). Can't embed Found into other workflows. Closed garden.
   - Evidence: Found official pages list only QuickBooks + bank sync integrations

3. **No Multi-Business**: Cannot manage 2+ LLCs / separate business entities in one account. Ceiling: solopreneurs, not micro-entrepreneurs with side hustles.
   - Evidence: Feature set limited to Schedule C (single business) across all documentation

## 💡 LESSONS FOR MMW

1. **Automatic Tax Set-Aside is Solopreneur Killer Feature**: Found's Taxes Pocket solves real anxiety ("Where is my Q1 tax money?"). MyMoneyWent should surface **"Estimated Tax Liability: $1,240 (set aside in separate Pocket)"** prominently. Link Telegram command: `/estimate-tax Q2` → returns auto-calculated estimate + "How much to save weekly" math.

2. **Business-Only Focus Simplifies Design**: Found avoids household/family complexity entirely. MMW's Telegram-first position could do same: **Solopreneur MVP = Personal+Business split only. No teams, no couples, no collaboration.** Adds 4-month dev cycle if team features needed; skip for launch.

3. **Integrated Banking = Data Advantage**: Found's bank account = perfect transaction source (zero sync delay). MMW via Telegram can't own bank account, but **prioritize Stripe/PayPal direct integrations over generic bank sync** (Plaid). Found proves users want "capture at source" not "sync after the fact."

4. **Closed Integrations Aren't Evil**: Found's "no Zapier, no API, no 3rd-party" stance is intentional. Simplifies support, reduces user confusion. MyMoneyWent could launch **CLI-only exports (CSV/PDF for accountants)**, no public API. Reduces scope, sharpens focus.

5. **Invoice Generation = Revenue Feature**: Found's free invoices are table-stakes for US solopreneurs. MyMoneyWent via Telegram can offer **simple invoice templates** (text-to-invoice), export to PDF/Stripe. But don't try to compete with Stripe Invoicing; stop at "basic needs."
