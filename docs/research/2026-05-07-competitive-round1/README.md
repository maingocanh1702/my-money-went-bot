## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS FOR MY MONEY WENT

1. **Product differentiation must address genuine pain** — Not feature parity (YNAB already owns zero-based; Monarch owns couples; Rocket owns subscriptions)
   - **Multicurrency addresses real pain:** YNAB users explicitly cite plugins needed; no competitor offers native solution

2. **Brand positioning must be clear and defensible** — "YNAB for travelers" or "Monarch for international users"
   - **Not:** "YNAB but cheaper" or "Monarch but better"

3. **CAC must be <$158 (3:1 LTV:CAC ratio)** at Year 1 to hit profitability path
   - **Organic (SEO) channels critical:** "multicurrency budgeting app," "budget app for expats," "freelancer budget tracker"
   - **Community-based growth:** Digital nomad networks, ex-pat Facebook groups, freelancer Slack communities

4. **Retention must hit >95% at Year 1** (vs industry 85-90%)
   - **Strategy:** Monthly "spending by currency" insights email; quarterly goal check-ins; community engagement (user meetups in cities)

5. **Pricing must resist compression** — Introduce secondary features (family privacy, business tracking, tax integration) at $129/$149/year tiers to maintain LTV
   - **Avoid race-to-bottom:** Price at $99/year, NOT $49

6. **Regulatory proactivity:** Consult FCA/PSD2 classification early (EU); confirm no "payment institution" license required

## IMMEDIATE NEXT STEPS (If proceeding with Multicurrency strategy)

1. **Weeks 1-2:** Validate with 50-100 target users (digital nomads, solopreneurs)
   - Survey: "Do you budget in multiple currencies? What's your current solution?"
   - Goal: 80%+ say yes and express pain with current solutions

2. **Weeks 3-4:** Build MVP mockups (Figma, InVision)
   - Core flows: Onboarding → Bank connect (Plaid) → Budget by currency → Dashboard
   - 3 currencies (USD, EUR, GBP) for MVP
   - Target 30-min onboarding time

3. **Weeks 5-8:** Technical validation (exchange rate APIs, Plaid integration)
   - Select provider (OpenExchangeRates, Exchangerate-api, Alpha Vantage)
   - Test real-time latency (<60s)
   - Confirm Plaid multicurrency support

4. **Weeks 9-12:** Launch closed beta
   - 100-500 early users (product hunt, reddit r/digitalnomad, hacker news, nomadlist)
   - Collect NPS, churn metrics, usage patterns
   - Iterate on UX based on feedback

5. **Months 4-6:** Open public launch (phased rollout by geography)
   - US (largest market, English-speaking)
   - EU (Open Banking maturity, ex-pat density)
   - Canada + Australia (English-speaking, Plaid coverage)

## KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY

**Market Consolidation:** Oligopoly emerging (YNAB + Rocket + Empower = 30M+ users). Premium tier (<$115/yr) saturated. Secondary tiers ($150+) and emerging markets underserved.

**Competitor Weaknesses:** 
- No multicurrency (all competitors avoid)
- Limited family privacy (all-or-nothing sharing)
- Android platform weaker than iOS
- Business+personal integration only at $199/yr (Monarch Plus)

**Defensible Opportunity:** Multicurrency-first positioning (400M+ TAM, <5% penetration, 6-12 month competitive response window)

**Pricing Strategy:** $99/year (YNAB parity) with $129/$149 premium tiers for secondary features (family privacy, business tracking)

**Team Size:** 6 FTE (1 PM, 2 backend, 1 mobile, 1 frontend, 1 devops/marketing) to launch MVP + first 10k users

**Timeline:** 12 months to MVP launch (Q3 2026), 24 months to profitability path ($4.95M ARR, 50k users)
