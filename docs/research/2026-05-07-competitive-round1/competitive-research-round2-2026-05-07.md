## KEY GAPS IDENTIFIED (Opportunities for MyMoneyWent)

### 1. **No Messaging/Chatbot UX**
- All 6 competitors use traditional mobile app UI
- **Zero precedent for Telegram bot-based personal finance**
- Massive differentiator opportunity

### 2. **Fragmented Auto-Capture**
- Spendee (Plaid but buggy) + Money Lover (Salt Edge, regional) only
- No app implements email/SMS parsing, OCR, or webhook
- **MyMoneyWent could own "smart parsing without Plaid" niche**

### 3. **No Business/Personal Accounting Split**
- All apps are personal-use only
- **Significant gap: no app addresses side-hustler/freelancer use case**
- Money Lover, Spendee support shared budgets (family), not business

### 4. **Subscription Pricing Volatility**
- 1Money switched from one-time to $7.99/mo (user backlash)
- Money Manager $5.99 one-time is rare (strong differentiator)
- **Most require $5/mo+ for bank sync**
- MyMoneyWent @ Free/$4/$9 aligns well, but must highlight **no recurring forced upgrades**

### 5. **Bank Sync Reliability Issues**
- Spendee users report frequent connection failures
- Money Lover sync is unreliable (stale data)
- **Opportunity: position MyMoneyWent as "manual + smart parsing" (no fragile Plaid dependency)**

### 6. **iOS App Discontinuation Risk**
- 1Money removed from iOS App Store (Dec 2024)
- **Indicates: cross-platform stability is competitive concern**
